Agapay helps you think, not copy

Agapay, the AI helper, only reads the topic you're on. Ask it to explain an idea, give a hint, help debug, check your understanding or break a task down, in English, Filipino or Taglish.

It guides your thinking and won't do your assignment for you. AI help has a daily limit of {{ ai_daily_limit }} messages, and resets every day.

Helping with: Loops in Python

Help me debug: my loop prints 0 to 4, but I want 1 to 5.

for i in range(5):
    print(i)

Hint 1 · a direction Look at where range() starts counting. What's the first number it gives you?

Hint 2 · a strategy range() can take two numbers: where to start and where to stop. The stop number is never included.

Hint 3 · a similar example range(3, 6) gives 3, 4 and 5. Which two numbers give 1 to 5?
